# The cities of this map: "city <row> <column>", on a hex that is a city (the
# letter c) in terrain.map. docs/map-format.md says what the lines mean.
#
# Four sides start with a city each, and a captain in it; their cities can
# build captains, to replace one that is lost. The other five cities are
# nobody's, each held by one of the weakest it builds. "income" is the gold a
# city brings its holder each turn: the sides' cities 40, the others 16 to 30
# (upkeep is half a unit's price).
# Gorsehaven trains its units: they come out veterans.
