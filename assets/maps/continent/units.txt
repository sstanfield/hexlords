# The kinds of unit of this map. docs/map-format.md says what the lines mean.
#
# "enter" is what a step onto each terrain costs, in movement points; a
# terrain that is left out cannot be entered. "road" and "bridge" are a step
# along a road, over land and over water.

# A commander: a captain makes every other unit of its stack fight 1 the
# stronger, and fights last itself. A stack has one.
unit captain
  name      Captain
  image     units/captain.png
  strength  3
  moves     14
  upkeep    4
  price     20
  commander 1
  enter     grass 2, forest 3, hill 4, swamp 5, desert 3
  enter     city 1, road 1, bridge 1

# Ships: over water, and into their own side's cities (ports). "carries" is
